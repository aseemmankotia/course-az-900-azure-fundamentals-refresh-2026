# Chapter 4 Lab: Azure Regions, Availability Zones, and Resource Organization

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Create a resource group in Azure portal with specific region, apply tags (Environment: Test, Department: IT), then explore subscription properties and view the management group structure

## Key takeaway

Availability zones protect against datacenter failure within a region, region pairs protect against entire region failure, and resource groups are lifecycle containers that cannot be nested.
