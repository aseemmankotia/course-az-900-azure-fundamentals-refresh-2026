# Chapter 2 Lab: Cloud Benefits: Availability, Scalability, and Reliability

**Estimated time:** 10 minutes

## Exercise

Navigate Azure Service Level Agreements page to compare SLA percentages for single-instance VMs, availability sets, and availability zones, then calculate allowed downtime per year

## Key takeaway

Elasticity means automatic scaling, high availability requires specific SLA percentages (99.99% = 53 minutes downtime/year), and predictability means knowing costs and performance in advance.
